# Example plans: source and license

The floor plan images in `img/` are from **CubiCasa5K**
(Kalervo, Ylioinas, Häikiö, Karhu, Kannala, 2019, https://zenodo.org/records/2613548),
licensed **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/),
as distributed in the Stoa Junction 2026 dataset, some with markings added there.

The files in `plans/` hold what the app read from these drawings (rooms, doors, windows,
fixtures) and the dataset's questions with their known answers. They are shared under the
same license.
